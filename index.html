Interactive Learning Curriculum

Master the mathematical mechanics powering modern generative AI
Lesson 1

1. Raw Text to Discrete Tokens

Why LLMs cannot read raw characters directly. Learn how Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) splits text into reusable subword chunks to balance vocabulary size against context length.

Lesson 2

2. Vocabulary & Token ID Indexes

How string chunks become integer indices. Explains lookup matrices, unknown token handling [UNK], and deterministic vocabulary indexing.

Lesson 3

3. High-Dimensional Vector Embeddings

Moving from arbitrary IDs to continuous geometrical space where concepts live as points. How King - Man + Woman = Queen works in vector arithmetic.

Lesson 4

4. Dimensionality Reduction (PCA & 3D Projection)

How Principal Component Analysis finds the primary axes of variance to mathematically collapse a 32-dimensional or 1536-dimensional space down to 3D.

Lesson 5

5. Cosine Similarity & Vector Metric

Why angle matters more than length in high-dimensional text representations: calculating normalized dot products and angular separation (θ).

Lesson 6

6. Scaled Dot-Product Self-Attention

How static embeddings become dynamic context-aware representations via Queries, Keys, Values, and the Softmax normalization kernel.

Lesson 7

7. Autoregressive Next-Token Prediction

How generative language models produce flowing paragraphs by iteratively appending the highest probability next token back into their context window.

Lesson 8

8. Temperature, Top-p & Sampling Strategies

Controlling hallucination versus creativity: how temperature flattens or sharpens softmax distributions during token generation.

About LLM Pipeline Explorer

LLM Pipeline Explorer is an interactive educational sandbox built to demystify how large language models (like GPT-4, Gemini, and Claude) ingest raw text and convert words into mathematical geometry.

Key Highlights:

  • Real 3D Three.js WebGL Scene: Rotate with left-click, pan with right-click, zoom with scroll wheel, and click nodes to highlight 5 nearest neighbors.
  • True Dimensionality Reduction (PCA): Computes real covariance matrices and power iteration in JavaScript to project 32D vectors into 3D space.
  • Deterministic Embedding Generator: Same words always receive the same continuous vector with realistic semantic cluster anchors.
  • Statistical Next-Word Engine: Computes real conditional n-gram probabilities from a built-in multi-domain corpus.

Designed with a crisp White and Orange aesthetic inspired by Linear, Stripe, and Apple design systems.
